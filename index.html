Grown-ups

Each lesson is about three minutes: meet a sound, find it among other letters, hear which pictures start with it, then blend sounds into words. The letter order follows UK phonics (s a t p, i n m d, g o c k, e u r, h b f l), the same order Theo will meet at school.

Pick the clearest letter sounds

Computer voices tend to say letter names ("tee") instead of letter sounds ("t"). Tap each number to hear a take, and keep the one that sounds like a short, clean sound. Your choice is saved on this device.

Test an activity

Pick a letter, then an activity to try it straight away. Test runs don't count towards Theo's progress or stickers.
